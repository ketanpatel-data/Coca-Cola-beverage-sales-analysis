# Coca-Cola Beverage Sales Analysis Using Excel

## Project Overview
This project analyzes beverage sales data across U.S. retailers to evaluate sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and pricing patterns. It includes an overall analysis of six beverage brands and a detailed drill-down into Coca-Cola.

## Business Objective
To analyze sales and profitability across beverage brands, retailers, and regions and identify opportunities for business improvement.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables and Formulas
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Conditional Formatting
- Excel Dashboard
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Data Preparation
- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Validated invoice dates and numeric fields.
- Created a Month helper column for monthly analysis.
- Prepared the data for PivotTables and visualizations.

## Analysis Performed
- Overall KPIs: Total Sales, Units Sold, Operating Profit, Operating Margin, and Average Price per Unit.
- Brand-wise sales and profitability analysis.
- Retailer-wise and region-wise performance analysis.
- State-level sales and operating margin analysis.
- Monthly sales trend analysis for 2021.
- Brand × Retailer, Brand × Region, and Retailer × Region analysis.
- Price per Unit vs Units Sold analysis.
- Sales vs Operating Profit and Sales vs Operating Margin analysis.
- Coca-Cola drill-down by retailer, region, state, and month.
- Excel dashboard combining key metrics and visualizations.

## Key Findings
- Total Sales: **$8.68 million**
- Total Units Sold: **17.15 million**
- Total Operating Profit: **$3.17 million**
- Overall Operating Margin: **36.55%**
- Coca-Cola generated the highest brand sales at approximately **$2.02 million**.
- Sodapop was the leading retailer, generating approximately **$4.40 million** in sales.
- The West was the highest-selling region, with approximately **$2.84 million** in sales.
- Sales and operating margin did not always move together across brands, retailers, and regions.
- Monthly sales peaked in July and December 2021.
- Higher-priced brands generally tended to have lower unit volumes, although the pattern was not consistent across every brand.

## Business Recommendations
- Evaluate sales and operating margin together when assessing performance.
- Investigate pricing, product mix, and operating costs where margins are comparatively low.
- Study the factors behind leading retailer and regional performance.
- Test targeted promotions or bundle offers for selected higher-priced products while monitoring profitability.
- Use monthly sales patterns to support inventory and promotional planning, validating them against additional years of data.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to prepare data, perform exploratory analysis, identify sales and profitability trends, and communicate business insights through a dashboard.

## Dataset Scope
The dataset covers the year 2021. Monthly trends therefore describe patterns within that year and do not establish long-term seasonality.
